Bridgle: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Bridgle is a word game for Android developed by MLB Solutions ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles and with whom it is shared.

No accounts, no personal data

Bridgle does not require an account and never asks for your name, email address or contacts. All gameplay data (bridges, results, streaks, achievements, hint balance, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app. We run no analytics and no advertising in this version.

Third-party services

  • Firebase Remote Config fetches app configuration (feature flags, daily puzzle overrides, minimum supported version). It uses a randomly generated installation identifier that is not tied to your identity. The game works fully offline if this request fails.
  • Google Play delivers the app and updates. Google's handling of install data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They are not sent from a server and use no personal data. You can turn them off in the app settings or in Android settings.

Friend challenges

A challenge link contains only an encoded puzzle reference and the sender's bridge words. It contains no name, account or device identifier. If you open the app for the first time from such a link, the app reads the Google Play install referrer once to start that challenge, then discards it. It is not stored or sent anywhere.

Children

Bridgle is a general-audience word game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

International data transfers

The services above are operated by Google and may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses where required. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

  1. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data stored on your device. Nothing else is retained by us.
  2. To ask about any data associated with Bridgle, email lstamenov.mlb@gmail.com with the subject "Bridgle data deletion request". We reply within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when optional leaderboards, ads or purchases are added, the updated version is published at this address before those features ship.
